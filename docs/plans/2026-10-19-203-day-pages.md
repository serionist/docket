# Add the day pages: Ledger, Queue, Meetings and Today

Four pages over the ledger in four PRs, sharing one item panel, one write route where a form post is one batch and its
Undo an undo of that batch, the old app's keys, and a reload when an op file changes. They read through #201's
`ledger/views.py` and write through `store.write`, `by: user`, `via: page`. The owner's old tasks app is the bar.

## Goal

- Done: what the old app does, on four pages: triage with a key per verdict, done, drop, snooze, reopen, edit, notes,
  materials, outcome, a log line, a new item; points added, answered, kept, dropped, removed, kept as proposed and
  turned into items; a meeting's outcome and notes; bulk and undo; a reload when anything else writes.
- Out: forget on the pages (#182); joining meetings by hand (Claude's `ledger_batch`); rollover and archive (the
  sync's, #210); streams' status (#208); the Outbox; the old app's lookup in harvest files (raw is in Search now).

## Design

### Built on

| From | What |
|---|---|
| #198, #199, #219 | `store.write`: one batch, all or none, `by` and `via` per op, undo by `of` or `batch`, an undone undo a redo (DR-0614, DR-0639) |
| #201's PR 2 | `views.read`: snapshot, show, queue, list, suggest; each row's `ref` is what a batch takes (DR-0638, DR-0643) |
| the web app | the shell, `page()`, `safely`, `read_form`, `history`, `#panel` swaps; plain posts on the writer thread with a 303 (DR-0381, DR-0473); Staging's state as hidden fields |

### The shared parts (PR 1)

| Part | How |
|---|---|
| state | one `DayState` for the four pages: `open`, `q`, `stream`, `kind`, `who`, `k`, `at`, `done`, then each page's own; one canonical URL per page |
| reads | `ledger_tables` in a thread beside the writer, then `views.read`; no fold, no query of the pages' own (DR-0616) |
| item panel | `day/_item.html` from `show`: the call, fields, its meeting's points, notes, materials, outcome, source, counts, history with an Undo per op (DR-0664) |
| writes | `POST /ledger/write`: a form is one batch, `item` repeated for a bulk; a 303 back with `done=<batch>` (DR-0661) |
| undo | the notice after a write shows the batch's log lines and Undo, an `undo` of the batch; after an undo, Redo |
| keys | 1 to 4 the verdicts, 0 no call, e done, Delete drop, s snooze, u undo, x tick, n new (DR-0662) |
| refresh | a poll every 5 s with a hash of the op and cursor files' names, sizes and times; changed, the page reloads (DR-0663) |
| search | `q`, every word folded, in an item's or meeting's text and log; `stream`, `kind`, `who` as `list` takes them (DR-0665) |

- `views.py` gains two reads, off the tool: `matching` (the ids `q` finds) and `batch_lines` (a batch's log lines,
  for the notice). Everything else the pages show comes from the tool's own views.
- The act bar is one form at the list's foot; each row's tick belongs to it (`form="act"`), and CSS shows it once a
  row is ticked. Its buttons: the four verdicts and no call, done, drop with its why, snooze (next working day,
  Monday, a week, two weeks, a day), reopen on closed rows.
- Keys act on the ticked rows, else the focused row, else the open item. After a write the focus goes to the row that
  followed the acted ones (`at`), as the old app's did; an open panel stays on its item.
- A write asks no index update: the next read's `ledger_tables` reads the grown file in (DR-0616).
- A refusal writes nothing and answers the page the form names, 400, the writer's words as its notice, text as typed.

### Each page

| Page | Reads | Lists, in order | Writes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Ledger `/ledger` | `list`, every state | `state`: open (default), snoozed, closed, all. Open by due (overdue, today, this week, later, none) or by `group` (stream, kind, who, meeting); snoozed by the day back; closed by its day, newest first; archived only with `archived=1` | act bar, item panel, New item |
| Queue `/queue` | `queue` | untriaged first, then discuss, claude, me, chase, each by due, none last | act bar, item panel, New item |
| Meetings `/meetings` | `snapshot` over `since` and `until`, 14 days back and ahead; `show`, `suggest` in the panel | by day from today, then earlier days, newest first; each day by start; cancelled and declined dimmed, and one with no points and no open items | meeting panel (DR-0667) |
| Today `/today` | `snapshot`, today to the next working day's end; cursor files, open runs | meetings left today, then the next working day's, not cancelled or declined, each with its open points and open items; due today and overdue; the last sync per source | both panels; act bar on due items |

- Within a group, rows go by due, opened, number. A list shows `k` rows, 100; Show more adds 100. Counts are over all.
- Awake: a snoozed item whose `until` is today or before counts as open and untriaged on every page, as todo.py's
  `visible_by_default`. The fold never reads the clock (DR-0615), so the read does (DR-0666). #201's `queue` gives
  `state = 'open'` only: its PR 2 takes these among the untriaged before it merges, or #204 does.
- The next working day is the next Monday to Friday in the owner's zone, as todo.py's.
- The meeting panel (PR 3): subject, time, organiser, where, response, attendees each linked to its brief when an
  entity names it; points and their buttons; add a point; keep or remove every proposed one; Suggest (`suggest=1`)
  with each item's why and an Add; outcome and notes; linked items; the recording; materials; history with Undo.

### What a button writes

| Button | Op, through `/ledger/write` |
|---|---|
| a verdict, no call | `triage` with `verdict`, or none, per item |
| done, its optional why and outcome; drop, its why required (DR-0611) | `done` or `drop` with `note` and `outcome` |
| snooze; reopen | `snooze` with `until`; `reopen` |
| the fields; notes; outcome; a log line | `set` of what changed; `notes` with `set`; `set` of `outcome`; `log` |
| a material added or removed | `material` with `ref` and `label`, or `remove` |
| New item | `add` with `as`, source `page:<day>T<hh:mm>` unless given; the page opens it |
| Undo on a history line | `undo` with `of`; none on an add |
| Undo, Redo in the notice | `undo` with `batch` |
| a point added, a suggestion added | `point` with `text`, and `item` |
| answer; answered or open; drop; reopen; text | `point_set` by the point's id: `answer`; `state`; `state` with `answer` cleared; `text` |
| keep one proposed; keep all | `point_set` clearing `proposed`; `keep` on the meeting |
| remove one; remove every proposed | `undo` of each `point` op, one batch |
| to an item | `point_item` |
| a meeting's outcome, notes | `set` on the meeting |

Rollover stays the sync's (DR-0624): a meeting that is over says its open points roll at the next sync.

### The source and materials

- A URL opens as a link. A locator opens Search's panel on its raw (`/search?hit=`), a recording the Recording page;
  a text locator naming no record links to Search with its text as the query. Each has Copy.
- A path from the host repo's top opens with its app through `POST /ledger/open`: only a material the item or meeting
  names, inside the host repo, a document, image or media type, nothing that runs or carries macros (DR-0664).

### Navigation and links

- Each PR marks its page built, first in "The day" (DR-0546); after PR 4: Today, Queue, Ledger, Meetings, Outbox later.
- From PR 4, `/` answers 303 to `/today` and the brand links to `/` (DR-0669).
- A person's brief links to `/ledger?who=<name>`; Today's sync line links each source to `/sync?source=<name>`; the
  Sync page doesn't change. The URLs only gain optional parameters, since the tasks skill (#209) opens them.

## Doors and data

None. The pages write only what the writer writes today, `by: user`, `via: page` (DR-0611): no new op, key, file or
tool parameter, and the two reads `views.py` gains stay off the `ledger` tool. Data: none.

## Risks

- A page load per key press: triaging 50 items is 50 loads of about a tenth of a second on loopback. Accepted;
  `hx-boost` if it drags.
- A read while a full index update holds the index waits up to `LEDGER_WAIT`, then folds in memory (DR-0619): slower,
  never wrong. Accepted.
- A panel left open writes notes over newer ones: the later set wins, the history keeps both and Undo brings the
  other back, as #198 accepted for two machines.
- Opening a material starts a process on the owner's desktop from a post: allow-listed types, inside the host repo,
  the post checked by the Guard (DR-0152). Accepted, as the old app did.
