# Cut the sync's last overheads

The third rehearsal read each recording once, but the sync still took 27 minutes against the old sync's 15, at about
52M cached tokens against 19M. Three overheads are left: forked source agents, a settle in many turns, and a summary
pieced together by shell. One PR.

## Goal

- Done: the next rehearsal, on the same window as the old sync, takes no longer than it, with fewer total tokens, and
  the main session goes from the last agent back to the summary's last line in under 3 minutes (Success, below).
- In: how the skill launches the source agents, what the draft quotes, `docket sync settle`, `docket sync summary`,
  the skill's settle and summary steps.
- Out: the block's labels and fields, the reconcile's rules for sure and unsure, the MCP tools, the recording agents'
  model trial (DR-0751), a source's own run.

## Design

Where the third rehearsal's main session spent its time, and what replaces it:

| Step | Third rehearsal | After |
|---|---|---|
| source agents | `fork`, Opus, each starting at 71.7k tokens and carrying them every turn | `general-purpose`, the session's model, the prompt file alone: about the recording agents' 43.7k |
| settle | 5.3 min: the draft read, about 20 `ledger show` calls and `suggest` four times, the whole draft written out again (an 11k-token turn), two dry runs and the write | the draft read once; one `docket sync settle` for every line a choice settles; an Edit only for a line that needs new words and for the prep points; one `suggest` |
| summary | about 3 min: Bash extractions from a snapshot, then a 6.8k-token turn | `docket sync summary`, read once and talked through |

### Why the main session called `show`

The draft's 25 unsure lines didn't carry what settling them needed:

| Calls | The unsure line | What it lacked |
|---|---|---|
| 5 meetings | MINUTES on an answered point, OUTCOME on a meeting with one: "keep one, or join them" | the answer and the outcome there, quoted cut at 72 characters, and no joined outcome to take |
| 1 meeting | MINUTES naming its point by number, `1`, not by id | the meeting's standing points |
| 2 streams | STREAMS whose pairs weren't fields of a stream | the stream's where, next and deadline |
| 2 items, a list | a log on the item a continues ref names, unsure since the title didn't look like it (11 lines) | what was last said on the item, and its due |
| 6 meetings, 1 item | the prep: the draft named the meetings by ref only | each meeting's open points, before new ones are worded |

`suggest` ran four times because 16 meetings were asked: 15 items each at about 700 characters a row with its JSON
fit about four meetings in the 40,000-character room, and the rest came back in `later`.

### Fresh source agents

- The skill launches each source agent `general-purpose`, with no `model`, so its judgment stays on the session's
  model; the recording agents keep `model: sonnet`.
- `agent.md` stands alone: the tools to load, the vault, the paths, the day and the zone were there already. It gains
  the owner, `{OWNER}`, their name and addresses from `vault.yaml`, since a fresh agent can't tell whose asks to find
  without it, and a line that another agent may be in the same browser. `recording.md` gains `{OWNER}` too.

### What the draft quotes

Each addition is a `# ?` line or a field at a candidate line's end: comment lines the writer skips.

| Unsure line | Gains |
|---|---|
| any with an item candidate | the candidate's line gains `due MM-DD` and `last MM-DD: <its latest log line>`, cut at 120 characters |
| MINUTES, the point answered or dropped | the point whole: `M-0001#2 answered | The budget | answer: 40k, agreed | <id>` |
| MINUTES, the point not standing there | the meeting's standing points, a line each; when the field is a number, `1` or `#1`, the op on that point as the alternative |
| OUTCOME, an outcome there or from an earlier line | that outcome whole, its lines joined by ` / `, and the two joined as the alternative |
| STREAMS | the stream's line whole: name, status, where, next, deadline |
| an add or a STREAMS line whose stream isn't held | the streams the ledger holds, by name |
| the `# prep` line | under it, each prep meeting's line and its open points, as `#` lines |

A quoted outcome or answer is cut at 1,000 characters, under the 2,000 at which the Read tool cuts a line. The perf
draft of 200 lines grows by about a line per unsure op.

### `docket sync settle <sync> <line>=<choice>...`

It settles the draft's unsure lines in one call, by the line numbers the reconcile printed and the Read tool shows,
and rewrites `draft.jsonl`. It writes nothing else.

| Choice | The line becomes |
|---|---|
| `keep` | the op, its `unsure` taken out, and the lines that go with it kept |
| `alt`, `alt2`, ... | its first, second, ... alternative, without its `# `; the lines that go with the op go too |
| `keep+alt2`, `alt+alt2` | those ops in that order: a merge kept and its other ask added too |
| `drop` | nothing, and the lines that go with it go too |

- A settled op's `# ?` lines and alternatives go with it, so the file holds only what is still open; the head line
  counts what is left.
- Refused, changing nothing: a line that isn't an unsure op, an alternative it doesn't have, a choice that isn't one
  of these, a sync of another vault.
- It prints the draft's path, its ops and the unsure lines left, by their new numbers, and the `# prep` line's.
- Not an Edit per line: 25 Edits are 25 calls, each quoting its line to be unique, and a merge's or an outcome's line
  is long; the rehearsal wrote the whole draft out instead. Not the batch tool taking choices: `ledger_batch` stays a
  door that only gains.

The skill's settle step:

1. Read the draft once.
2. Pick the prep meetings from the `# prep` lines and ask `suggest` once, with `refs`.
3. `docket sync settle` with every line a choice settles.
4. When a line needs new words, or for the prep points: Read the lines `settle` names again, then an Edit each, the
   prep points in one Edit at `# prep`.
5. The dry run, then the write, as now.

`show` only when a candidate's quoted line can't answer the question.

### `docket sync summary <sync>`

Read-only: it prints the summary's groups from the ledger after the write, one line per item in the short form.

| Group | What |
|---|---|
| the batch | this sync's batches by `sync`, each with its undo |
| closed, added | from the batch: each done or drop with its line, each add's item; how many logs on how many items |
| meetings and prep | the meetings from now to the fifth working day with an open point or an open item, each point marked `proposed` where it is, and the outcomes the batch wrote |
| today and overdue | what the owner owes, due today or before |
| this week | what they owe, due in the next 7 days |
| whenever | the rest they owe |
| invites, waiting | the open invites; what they wait on, with its due and how late |
| left over | past meetings still holding an open point that rollover had nowhere to take |
| runs short | each RUN line whose mark didn't move or whose run didn't open, and each source with no block |
| notes | each recording given an agent, its note put or not by the index |
| staging | the records waiting on the Staging page |

- An item is listed once among today, this week, whenever, invites and waiting; a meeting's items may repeat it.
- It keeps within 30,000 characters, what a Bash result keeps: past it, whenever is cut first, then left over, each
  to its count and `docket ledger list`.
- A batch is this sync's when its op id's time is after the sync's start and it was written `by: sync`: ops' ids are
  minted from the clock (DR-0612), so no file in the sync folder has to record the write.

## Risks

| Risk | Resolved or accepted |
|---|---|
| A fresh agent misses what the fork knew from the session | `agent.md` names everything it needs, the owner now too; general-purpose agents get the project's `CLAUDE.md` as any session does |
| Settle by number on a draft that changed since it was read | settle refuses a number that isn't an unsure op, and prints the new numbers after |
| The quotes make the draft too big to Read whole | each quote is cut; a quote comes only with an unsure line |
| The summary misses a batch written outside the sync | it takes every batch by `sync` after the start, and names each with its undo |
| Prep still asks for many meetings and `later` comes back | the skill picks the prep meetings first from the `# prep` lines; `later` is still read until empty |
