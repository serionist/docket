# Keep a name's common stem forms when its own case forms explain them

## Goal

A customer named in most chunks keeps its Hungarian inflected mentions. Today `matching.py` drops a stem form in over
a fifth of the chunks and over 100 (DR-0116), so in a one-customer vault `Acme` keeps the chunks saying "Acme" and
loses those saying only "Acménél" or "Acmét": `Acme` and `Acménél` both stem to `acm`, and `acm` is in most chunks.
`Acme-nak` is found today, through its written part.

- In scope: the fifth-of-the-chunks rule in `judge_forms`; Hungarian's case forms as a language hook; the schema
  version; tests on an invented vault; DR-0116 superseded.
- Out of scope: the ratio rule (a form in over ten times as many chunks as the name as written), which still drops
  `rob` while `Rob` is rare and `robot` is everywhere; the English words the Hungarian stemmer cuts to a name's form
  below a fifth of the chunks (`same` for Sam, `marked` for Mark), which the ratio rule alone judges, as today;
  possessive and plural forms (`Acméje`, `Acméék`).

## Design

A form in over a fifth of the chunks and over 100 is kept when nine in ten of its chunks hold the alias as written or
in one of its case forms; otherwise it is dropped, as today. Below that share nothing changes.

| Module | Change |
|---|---|
| `index/languages.py` | `Language.cases`, and `hungarian_cases(word)`: the word, its last vowel lengthened as for the dative, followed by each case ending |
| `index/matching.py` | `judge_forms`: for a form past the share, one count of its chunks holding none of the alias's written or case forms (`stem_hu : "acm" NOT (folded : "acme" OR folded : "acmenel" OR ...)`); dropped when over a tenth of its chunks |
| `index/schema.py` | `SCHEMA_VERSION` 3, since `alias_terms` and `mentions` change |

- Case endings: `-nak/-nek`, `-nál/-nél`, `-hoz/-hez/-höz`, `-ban/-ben`, `-ba/-be`, `-ból/-ből`, `-ra/-re`,
  `-ról/-ről`, `-tól/-től`, `-ig`, `-ért`, `-ként`, `-ul/-ül`; after a vowel `-t`, `-n`, `-val/-vel`, `-vá/-vé`; after
  a consonant `-t`, `-ot/-at/-et/-öt`, `-on/-en/-ön`, and the consonant doubled before `-al/-el` and `-á/-é`
  (`Markkal`; a digraph doubles its first letter, `Kováccsal`).
- Harmony: back endings for a word with a back vowel, front for one with a front vowel or none, both for one with
  both, since foreign names go either way (`Acménak`, `Acmének`). Folding hides the lengthened vowel, so `Acménél`
  matches as `acmenel`.
- Only the alias's last word takes endings (`Northwind Tradersnél`), as its stem forms do now.
- A query word that is a kept form names the entity, so `acménél` in a query now reads as Acme, as `acme` does.

Measured on invented vaults built straight into an index, English and Hungarian mixed; the counts are chunks found
of the true ones, and wrong ones:

| Rule for a form past the share | Acme in 60% | Mark, `már` in 45% | Mark, `már` in 15% | Sam, `same` in 25% | Mark, English `marked`, `market` in 25% |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| dropped (today) | 380 of 600 | 0 wrong | 0 wrong | 0 wrong | 0 wrong |
| the ratio rule alone | 600 of 600 | 370 wrong | 110 wrong | 230 wrong | 230 wrong |
| a fifth of the chunks without the written alias | 380 of 600 | 0 wrong | 110 wrong | 0 wrong | 0 wrong |
| explained by any word starting with the alias | 600 of 600 | 0 wrong | 110 wrong | 230 wrong | 230 wrong |
| a fifth of the chunks its case forms don't explain | 600 of 600 | 0 wrong | 110 wrong | 0 wrong | 0 wrong |
| a tenth of its chunks its case forms don't explain (this plan) | 600 of 600 | 0 wrong | 0 wrong | 0 wrong | 0 wrong |

- Mark and Sam are named in 8% to 16% of the chunks, so the ratio rule lets their forms through and only the share
  judges them. Where it drops them, their own `Marknak` and `Sammal` chunks are lost, as today.
- On real English prose (the standard library's docstrings in 350-word chunks), 28 of the 102 words in over a fifth of
  the chunks are cut by the Hungarian stemmer to their own first letters: `same` to `sam`, `name` to `nam`, `data` to
  `dat`. So "starts with the alias" can't tell a name from a word; a case ending can.
- Cost: on 100,000 chunks with four such entities, `judge_forms` took 78 ms against 62; only a form past the share pays
  its one count. `mentions` took 422 ms against 312, from Acme's extra rows.

### Decided

Taken under the autonomy grant, `proposed` for the maintainer's review.

| Decided | Not taken | Entry |
|---|---|---|
| a form past the share is kept when nine in ten of its chunks hold the alias as written or in a case form | the ratio rule alone; the share over chunks without the written alias; any word starting with the alias; a fifth of the unexplained chunks (the table above); a capital letter as the sign of a name: chat is often lower case | DR-0310 |
| Hungarian's case forms generated from a list of endings, by harmony, after a vowel or a consonant | a prefix query: `same` starts with `sam`; the stemmer itself: it cuts `same` and `Samnek` alike | DR-0310 |
| DR-0310 restates and supersedes DR-0116, which this rule changes | a second entry beside it: DR-0116's share sentence would be wrong | DR-0310 |
| schema version 3, so every index rebuilds once | no bump: an index would keep the old judging until its next change; #59's version 2 is unreleased, so users rebuild once for both | DR-0310 |

## Doors and data

None of AGENTS.md's one-way doors; entity files (DR-0113) are read as before. Nothing in the vault is written.
`SCHEMA_VERSION` goes from 2 to 3 (or to the next free one if #46 merges first), so an index rebuilds once at its next
update, as DR-0115 has it. #59's bump to 2 is in the same unreleased section, so users see one rebuild.

## Steps

1. `Language.cases` and `hungarian_cases`. Test: `test_index_text.py`.
2. `judge_forms` judges a form past the share by its explained chunks; `SCHEMA_VERSION` 3; `docs supersede DR-0116
   --by DR-0310`, and `matching.py`'s docstring cites DR-0310. Tests: `test_index_matching.py` below;
   `test_index_update.py`'s stale-schema row reads "schema 2, and this Docket writes 3".
3. The docs below, then `finish-work`: `uv run gate full`, a draft PR, `review-code` until it is clean.

## Tests

On temp vaults made with `Vault.init`, temp config and state dirs, the failing keyring and `DOCKET_VAULT` unset, as
`conftest.py` sets them; invented names only.

A one-customer vault, `en` and `hu`, 400 messages, built once per module through `template_with`: Acme in 270 (100
bare, 110 only inflected, 20 `Acme-nak`, 40 English "Acme's"), Mark in 60 with `Már` in 150, Sam in 60 with "the same"
in 70.

| Test | Asserts |
|---|---|
| `test_index_text.py`: `test_hungarian_case_forms_follow_harmony_and_assimilation` | `Acme` gives `acménak` and `acmének`, `acmét`, `acmével`; `Mark` gives `markkal`, `markot`, `marknak`, not `market` or `marked`; `Kovács` gives `kováccsal`; `Péter` takes front endings only; `2026` none |
| `test_a_name_in_most_chunks_keeps_its_inflected_mentions` | all 270 Acme chunks mention it, the 110 inflected ones included; `acm` is kept; a search for `acménél` reads the entity Acme |
| `test_a_common_word_sharing_a_name_s_stem_is_still_dropped` | `mar` and `sam` are dropped as common; no chunk saying only "Már kész" mentions Mark, none saying only "the same crane" mentions Sam |

- The first fails on main (160 of 270) and with the share over chunks without the written alias.
- The second fails with the ratio rule alone (Mark 150 wrong, Sam 70), with any word starting with the alias (Sam 70)
  and with a fifth of the unexplained chunks (Sam 70): each alternative in the table fails one of the two.
- `test_short_and_flooded_stem_forms_are_dropped_and_listed` stays as it is: its vault is under 100 chunks, so only
  the ratio rule judges there.
- Coverage stays at the `docket` floor of 100%.

## Docs

- DR-0310 in `packages/docket/index.yaml`, added with this plan; it supersedes DR-0116 in step 2.
- `docs/design.md`, Keywords and aliases: a form that floods the index is dropped unless nine in ten of its chunks hold
  the name or one of its case forms, so a customer in most chunks keeps `Acménél`.
- `CHANGELOG.md`, Changed: a customer or person named in most of your notes is now found in its Hungarian inflected
  forms too (`Acménél`, `Acmét`); folded into #59's rebuild line when both are unreleased.

## Risks

| Risk | Status |
|---|---|
| A common word that is also a case form of the name (`robot` is `Rob` with `-ot`) counts as the name | accepted: the ratio rule drops it while the name is rare as written, and in Hungarian it reads both ways |
| A name mostly written in forms the list lacks (possessives, `Acméje`) has over a tenth unexplained and is dropped | accepted: today's result; those forms mostly stem apart (`acme`, `acmee`) |
| English words the Hungarian stemmer cuts to a name's form, in under a fifth of the chunks, already count as the name when the ratio lets them (`same` for Sam) | accepted: today's behaviour, outside this rule; worth an issue of its own |
| Every index rebuilds once | accepted: in the same release as #59's rebuild |
