# Count a name's stem form only where its case forms explain it

## Goal

An English word the Hungarian stemmer cuts like a name stops counting as that name. Today, in a vault with English and
Hungarian, every chunk saying "the same" mentions Sam and every "marked" or "market" mentions Mark: `same` stems to
Sam's `sam`, `marked` and `market` to Mark's `mar`, and #60's check of the name's case forms runs only for a form in
over a fifth of the chunks. A query reads the same way: "the same crane" reads as Sam, and `same` leaves the search.

- In scope: the mention rule in `matching.py`, for chunks and for search's entity groups; how `query.py` reads a name
  in a query; the share rule, which the check per chunk replaces; languages without case forms; a time limit for the
  mention pass; the schema version; DR-0310 superseded.
- Out of scope: a case form that is itself a word (`bent` is Ben with `-t`); new forms in the case list (possessives,
  plurals); `asr` misspellings.

## Design

A kept stem form counts only in a chunk that also holds the alias as written or in one of its case forms. `expressions()`
ANDs an alias's stem forms in a column with #60's `explained()`, so the case list stays in one place:

`folded : "sam" OR (stem_hu : "sam" AND (folded : "sam" OR folded : "samnak" OR folded : "sammal" OR ...))`

| Module | Change |
|---|---|
| `index/matching.py` | `expressions(conn, analyzer, ...)` wraps each column's stem forms as `(forms AND explained)`, and leaves the `asr` misspelling phrases as they are; `judge_forms` loses the share branch (`COMMON_SHARE`, `COMMON_MIN`, `UNEXPLAINED_ONE_IN`): a form is short, common by the ratio rule, or kept |
| `index/query.py` | `entity_group` passes the analyzer, so a search for Sam fetches no "the same" chunk; `_lookups` keeps each stem key's alias, and `entity_spans` takes a hit through a stem form only when the query word is that alias's last word or one of its case forms, computed for the hit alone |
| `index/languages.py` | `alias_stems` goes: a language matches aliases on stems when it gives `cases`, which only Hungarian does, as today |
| `index/schema.py` | `SCHEMA_VERSION` 5, since `alias_terms` and `mentions` change |

- Per chunk, not per form. #60's check run for every form drops a whole form for its stray chunks: Sam's `sam` goes, and
  every `Sammal` with it. The check per chunk keeps `Sammal` and drops only "the same".
- The share rule goes. Past a fifth of the chunks the check per chunk does its work without dropping the form, so Mark
  keeps `Marknak` while `már` is in a third of the chunks. The ratio rule stays: it keeps `robot`, a case form of Rob,
  from counting while Rob is rare.
- A language without case forms gets no fallback to today's rule: only the written alias would explain its chunks, so a
  stem form adds nothing. Tying stems to `cases` makes that state impossible instead of silent.
- English possessives and plurals: "Sam's" is the words `sam` and `s`, so it counts as written. "Sams" and "Marks" stem
  apart (`sams`, `marks`) and count neither today nor after.
- An alias with `needs` gets the same expression, then its condition. `asr` misspellings aren't checked: a misheard name
  holds no written form (`Nortvinnál` for `Nortwin`), and misspellings start from five letters, so Sam and Mark have
  none.

Measured on invented vaults built straight into an index, English and Hungarian mixed: 1,000 chunks with Sam, Mark,
Bálint and Acme, and #60's one-customer vault of 400. The counts are chunks found of the true ones; wrong ones only
where there are any:

| Rule | Sam, "the same" in 8% | Mark, "marked", "market" in 10% | Acme in 60% | #60's Mark, `már` in 38% | #60's Sam, "the same" in 18% |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| today | 80 of 80, 71 wrong | 70 of 70, 100 wrong | 600 of 600 | 50 of 60 | 40 of 60 |
| #60's check for every form | 60 of 80 | 40 of 70 | 580 of 600 | 50 of 60 | 40 of 60 |
| per chunk, the share kept | 80 of 80 | 60 of 70 | 580 of 600 | 50 of 60 | 40 of 60 |
| per chunk, the share gone (this plan) | 80 of 80 | 60 of 70 | 580 of 600 | 60 of 60 | 60 of 60 |

- The 10 Mark and 20 Acme chunks lost name them only in a possessive (`Markéhoz`, `Acméje`): today's stem forms reach
  those, the case forms don't.
- Bálint, only inflected (`Bálintnak`, `Bálinttal`), is found in all 50 chunks under every rule.
- Cost, on the perf index (100,000 chunks, 300 entities, 400 aliases): `judge_forms` 16 ms before and after,
  `mentions` 531 ms against 547. With 60 more names made of the index's commonest stems, so each AND reads a long
  list: `mentions` 1,203 ms both ways. Nothing in the repo limits the pass today; this plan adds a limit of 2 s.

### Decided

Taken under the autonomy grant, `proposed` for the maintainer's review.

| Decided | Not taken | Entry |
|---|---|---|
| a kept stem form counts only in a chunk holding the alias as written or in a case form, whatever its share | the check past a fifth only, as today; #60's check for every form: Sam loses `Sammal` (the table above) | DR-0350 |
| the share rule goes; the short and ratio rules stay | the share kept beside it: `már` still costs Mark `Marknak`; the ratio gone too: `robot` would count as Rob | DR-0350 |
| a query word names an entity through a stem form only as one of the alias's case forms | the query read as today: "the same crane" reads Sam, and `same` leaves the search | DR-0350 |
| a language matches aliases on stems only when it gives case forms; `alias_stems` goes | today's rule as a fallback: the leak again; stem forms judged and listed but never counting | DR-0350 |
| `asr` misspellings stay unchecked; possessives and plurals stay out of the case forms | checking misspellings: `Nortvinnál` holds no form of `Nortwin`; `-é` among the case forms: `Samé` folds to `same` | DR-0350 |
| the perf test holds `judge_forms` and `mentions` on its index under 2 s, printed under coverage | no limit, as today; the queries' 1 s: the pass runs on an update, not on a search | none |
| DR-0350 restates and supersedes DR-0310; schema version 5, so every index rebuilds once | a second entry beside DR-0310, whose share sentence would be wrong; no bump: an index keeps the old mentions until its next change | DR-0350 |

## Doors and data

None of AGENTS.md's one-way doors; entity files (DR-0113) are read as before, and nothing in the vault is written.
`SCHEMA_VERSION` goes from 4 (#46's) to 5, or to the next free one at merge, so an index rebuilds once at its next
update, as DR-0115 has it. #59's, #60's and #46's bumps are in the same unreleased section, so users see one rebuild.

## Steps

1. The rule: `expressions()` with `explained()`, the share branch gone, `alias_stems` replaced by `cases`,
   `SCHEMA_VERSION` 5; `docs supersede DR-0310 --by DR-0350`, and `matching.py`'s docstring cites DR-0350. Tests: the
   mention tests and `test_index_text.py` below.
2. The query: `_lookups` and `entity_spans` in `query.py`. Test: the query test below.
3. The limit: the mention pass timed in `test_index_perf.py`.
4. The docs below, then `finish-work`: `uv run gate full`, a draft PR, `review-code` until it is clean.

## Tests

On temp vaults made with `Vault.init`, temp config and state dirs, the failing keyring and `DOCKET_VAULT` unset, as
`conftest.py` sets them; invented names only.

A team vault, `en` and `hu`, 400 messages, built once per module through `template_with`, no form past a fifth: Sam in
40 (`Sam`, `Sammal`, "Sam's"), "the same" in 31; Mark in 28 (`Mark`, `Marknak`, `Markkal`), "marked" and "market" in
40; Bálint in 20, only inflected (`Bálintnak`, `Bálinttal`).

| Test | Asserts |
|---|---|
| `test_index_matching.py`: `test_an_english_word_stemming_like_a_name_is_no_mention` | Sam, Mark and Bálint are mentioned in exactly their own chunks: `Sammal`, "Sam's", `Marknak` and every Bálint chunk in, none saying only "the same", "marked" or "market" |
| `test_index_matching.py`: `test_a_query_word_stemming_like_a_name_stays_a_word` | "the same crane" reads no entity and keeps `same` as a word; `sammal` reads Sam; a search for Sam returns no chunk saying only "the same" |
| `test_index_matching.py`: `test_a_common_word_sharing_a_name_s_stem_never_counts_as_the_name`, #60's test renamed | in the one-customer vault `mar` and `sam` are kept, and Mark and Sam are mentioned in all their chunks, `Marknak` and `Sammal` included, and in none saying only "Már kész" or "the same" |
| `test_index_text.py`: `test_only_languages_with_case_forms_match_aliases_on_stems` | with `en`, `hu` and `de`, only `hu` gives stem forms |
| `test_index_perf.py`: `test_the_mention_pass_stays_under_two_seconds` | `judge_forms` and `mentions` on the perf index, in a transaction rolled back, under 2 s when untraced |

- The first two fail on main: every "the same" chunk mentions Sam, every "marked" one Mark, and "the same crane"
  reads Sam. The third fails on main and with the share kept (Mark 50 of 60, Sam 40 of 60).
- Step 1's rule was run as an uncommitted patch against the whole suite: every test passed but #60's renamed one, the
  `asr` test (`Nortvinnál`), the `needs` test and the eval set at its floors among them.
- Coverage stays at the `docket` floor of 100%.

## Risks

| Risk | Status |
|---|---|
| A case form that is itself a word counts as the name: `bent` is Ben with `-t`, `robot` Rob with `-ot`, `samba` Sam with `-ba` | accepted: the ratio rule drops the form while the word is in over ten times as many chunks as the name; on the invented vault "bent" gave Ben 57 wrong mentions, today and after |
| A chunk naming someone only in a possessive or plural (`Markéhoz`, `Acméje`, `Katák`) loses the mention today's stem forms give it | accepted: 10 of Mark's 70 and 20 of Acme's 600 above; `-é` can't join the case forms (`Samé` folds to `same`), `-ja` and `-je` could in a later issue |
| A query typed in such a form (`markéhoz`) no longer reads as the name | accepted: the rule a chunk follows; the word is still searched as a word |
| A flooded stem form is kept now, so its AND reads a long list on every update | accepted: measured above, and the perf test holds the pass under 2 s |
| Every index rebuilds once | accepted: in the same release as #59's, #60's and #46's rebuilds |
