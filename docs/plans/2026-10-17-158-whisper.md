# Transcribe a recording with Whisper next to the recorder's transcript

## Goal

A recording whose audio is stored gets a second transcript, from Whisper large-v3 on this machine. It is kept and
indexed next to the recorder's own. The design's test on 38 minutes of work calls found the two make different
mistakes, so keeping both finds more names and numbers (design §8).

| The issue's part | This plan | PR |
|---|---|---|
| Engine | faster-whisper 1.2.1 on CTranslate2 4.8.2, float16 on CUDA, int8 on a CPU, no torch, in an environment of its own; measured on the RTX 5090: 26x real time | 1 |
| Install | `docket models install --speech`, off by default; on a CPU it warns, and transcribes only when asked | 1 |
| Pipeline | the stored, cut audio only; #173's child decodes it to 16 kHz PCM; Silero VAD; the language forced where known; a screened vocabulary prompt for Hungarian | 1 |
| Record | `<recording rid>/whisper`, a transcript with its engine, version, language and segments, staged through the gate and cut by the family's ranges | 1 |
| Tool | `docket transcribe <rid>` (PR 1); MCP `transcribe(rid)`, the service's queue, and a transcript for each recording whose audio is stored (PR 2) | 1, 2 |
| Search | both transcripts chunked; hits on one stretch collapse into one group, by the record's thread | 2 |
| Measured | `docket eval speech <file>`: word, character and name error rates on hand-corrected stretches kept outside the repo; CI runs an invented clip on tiny | 1, 2 |

Out of scope: reconciling the two transcripts and learning from corrections (design §8, steps 5 and 6), diarization,
and Apple's GPU (whisper.cpp or mlx), which stays a CPU run until measured on a Mac.

## Design

### The engine, measured

On the maintainer's desktop (RTX 5090, driver 617.42, 32 logical cores), on an invented English clip made by Windows'
own speech synthesis, 53 s repeated to 11 minutes with 2 s of digital silence between, beam 5, VAD on:

| Run | Speed | Load |
|---|---|---|
| large-v3, float16, CUDA | 657 s of audio in 25.5 s: 26x real time, an hour in about 2.5 minutes | 2.3 s |
| large-v3, float32, CUDA | 23x | 5.0 s |
| large-v3, int8, CPU, 8 threads | 1.7x | 4.5 s |
| large-v3, int8, CPU, 16 threads | 1.0x | 4.2 s |
| tiny, int8, CPU, 4 threads | 44x, every key word of the clip right | 0.2 s |

The clip's names came back right; its WER of 0.14 is mostly "42 tons" for "forty two tonnes".

| Option | Why not |
|---|---|
| ONNX Runtime with the onnx-community fp16 export | it would reuse the CUDA environment, but the decoding loop (timestamp rules, the KV cache, fallbacks, VAD) is 400 to 600 lines of our own, against faster-whisper's tested ones |
| onnxruntime-genai | a second runtime, and its Whisper example forces English and no timestamps; prompts and long audio unproven |
| large-v3-turbo | the design's test found it drops numbers (design §8) |

CTranslate2's wheels are built against CUDA 12, so the CUDA environment takes `nvidia-cublas-cu12` (12.9.2.10, about
0.9 GB installed) beside it. That, and faster-whisper's own `onnxruntime` CPU package for the VAD, which would clash
with `onnxruntime-gpu`, are why speech gets an environment of its own: `models/env-speech-<device>`, built as the
embedder's is (DR-0332), from hashed `requirements-speech-cuda.txt` and `-cpu.txt` in the wheel: ctranslate2 4.8.2,
faster-whisper 1.2.1, av 18.1.0 (Docket's own pin), tokenizers and onnxruntime as faster-whisper asks, and cuBLAS on
CUDA, its DLL folder added before ctranslate2 loads. int8 isn't used on CUDA: it failed on RTX 50 cards (CTranslate2
#1865).

Models, from Hugging Face at pinned revisions with their sha256 (DR-0333), MIT:

| Model | Repo, revision | Size | For |
|---|---|---|---|
| large-v3 | Systran/faster-whisper-large-v3 @ edaa852ec7e1 | 3.09 GB | every user |
| tiny | Systran/faster-whisper-tiny @ d90ca5fe2602 | 76 MB | CI's smoke test, `--speech tiny` |

### Install

`docket models install --speech` builds the speech environment for the device and fetches large-v3; `--speech tiny`
fetches tiny. It is off by default and never part of a plain `docket models install`. On a CPU it says that an hour of
audio takes about an hour and that it transcribes only when asked; on CUDA it times the invented clip once, as the
reranker is timed (DR-0455), and writes the time into the environment's marker. `docket models status` shows the
speech model, the device and that time; `docket models remove --speech` removes its environment and files. The user
config's models block gains `speech`: a catalogue name, or none.

### The pipeline

| Step | Where | What |
|---|---|---|
| 1. checks | the core, under the lock | the reader's state is `ok`: audio stored, cut by every range its family needs (#157), not held; the gate's checks on a transcript of the recording |
| 2. decode | #173's child, a new `pcm` mode | the stored file decrypted into the per-process plain folder, decoded to 16 kHz mono float32, written beside it; both plain files go on every path |
| 3. transcribe | the speech worker, one process per job | VAD (faster-whisper's Silero v6), Whisper in pieces of up to 30 minutes cut at a VAD gap, timestamps offset to the recording's timeline |
| 4. record | the core | segments overlapping the family's cut ranges dropped, the ranges cut again (`cuts.apply`), the record staged through the gate |

- The worker runs `python -I -X utf8 speech_worker.py '<spec>'` in the speech environment with the stripped
  environment (DR-0330): the PCM file, the model folder, the device, the compute type, the language, the prompt and the
  VAD settings. It answers JSON lines: progress by milliseconds done, each segment (start, end, text, its average log
  probability and no-speech probability), then `done` or an error by type only. Its stderr is kept, never logged.
- Settings: `vad_filter`, Silero's 0.5 threshold, faster-whisper's 2 s minimum silence and 400 ms padding;
  `condition_on_previous_text=False`; beam 5; the 0.6 no-speech, -1.0 log-probability and 2.4 compression thresholds.
  The cut stretches are digital silence, so VAD drops them; step 4 drops anything Whisper still put there.
- Language: the family's transcript's `lang` when it has one, else the vault's only language, else Whisper detects it
  from the first 30 s. Forced, a stretch in the other language comes back translated: accepted, as one file has one
  language.
- Hungarian gets a vocabulary prompt: "Résztvevők és témák:" and up to 40 names, about 120 tokens at most. The names
  are the family's speakers resolved to entities, those entities' related projects, and the entities whose name or an
  alias appears in the family's records, by their names and `asr` aliases. Each name is screened before it goes in:
  one a never-store list matches, or one the redaction rules would change, is left out, since Whisper can repeat a
  prompt's words, and a never-store value in the transcript would drop it and withhold the recording's family. The
  prompt goes only to the local worker and is never logged.

### The record

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| rid | `<recording rid>/whisper`, so it sits in the recording's family: its media checks, holds and withheld marks cover it |
| kind, source, container, thread | `transcript`, the recording's source, the recording's rid twice |
| ts | the recording's |
| segments, text | Whisper's, cut, with the text rendered from them |
| lang, engine | the language used; `whisper-large-v3@edaa852 faster-whisper-1.2.1` |
| fidelity, via | `derived`, `docket transcribe` |

It goes through `Gate.stage` like any record: redaction, never-store, holds, the release's cuts and the source's
sensitivity, so a mixed source's transcript waits for the owner. A new engine or model revision makes a new version;
the same engine again on the same audio stages nothing new. Never from uncut audio: the reader's `ok` means the stored
file holds every range, and step 4 cuts again by the same ranges.

### The tool and the queue (PR 2)

- MCP `transcribe(rid)`: queued in the service, one job at a time, answering at once with the job's place. Its outcome
  shows in the service's status and the Recording page: queued, running, staged, or why not.
- `docket transcribe <rid>` asks the running service, or runs the job itself when none runs.
- After each index update the service queues, on CUDA only, the newest 20 recordings whose audio reads `ok` and that
  have no Whisper transcript staged or in raw: so a sync's `media_put`, once promoted, gets its transcript. On a CPU
  nothing is queued by itself.

### Search (PR 2)

Each transcript is chunked as transcripts are. The Whisper record's thread is the recording, as Plaud's transcript's
is, so its chunks share the recording's group, and `query.py` already collapses hits of one group whose times overlap
by more than half: a stretch both transcripts match shows once. A test holds it.

### Measured (PR 1 for CI, PR 2 for the command)

- CI: the Models jobs install `--speech tiny` on the CPU and transcribe an invented 10 s clip the test makes with the
  system's own speech synthesis (SAPI on Windows, `say` on macOS, espeak-ng on Linux, installed in the job). It asserts
  key words come back and that a cut stretch has no words.
- `docket eval speech <file>`: the file, kept outside the repo like the question file (DR-0290), lists stretches
  (`rid`, start, end, the corrected text, the names in it). It prints, per engine, WER and CER after lowercasing and
  stripping punctuation, keeping diacritics, and a name error rate: the share of listed names missing from the
  stretch, accents and case folded, with the names it added that the corrected text lacks.

## Tests

Invented data only: records from the test kit, tones and synthesized speech made at test time, a fake worker for the
protocol. No real recording, here or in CI.

| Test | Proves |
|---|---|
| a held, recut, unstored or never-store recording | refused before anything decodes |
| a release cut and a hand cut | the record's segments have `[cut]` where the audio is silent, and none of Whisper's words there |
| a worker that hangs, crashes or answers junk | stopped, nothing staged, plain files gone |
| the prompt | names from speakers, related projects and mentions; one a never-store list matches and one redaction changes are left out; 40 at most |
| the record | its rid, engine, language and segments; a second run stages nothing; a new revision stages a new version |
| (CI) tiny on an invented clip | key words back; no words in a cut stretch |
| (PR 2) the queue | one job at a time; a CPU machine queues nothing by itself; the MCP tool's schema |
| (PR 2) search | a stretch both transcripts match is one hit |
| (PR 2) eval | WER, CER and name error rate on an invented file and transcript |

## Risks

| Risk | Status |
|---|---|
| CTranslate2 is CUDA 12 only; a future driver may drop CUDA 12 | accepted: cuBLAS 12 comes in the speech environment; an ONNX route stays the fallback |
| sm_120 fp16 runs through PTX; measured at 26x, enough | resolved by the measurement |
| Hungarian accuracy is unmeasured here: the clip is English | the maintainer's hand-corrected stretches and `docket eval speech` |
| the prompt makes Whisper repeat names into silence | VAD, `condition_on_previous_text=False` and the cut's drop; measured with and without the prompt |
| a forced language translates the other language's stretches | accepted for one-language files; detection for files whose language isn't known |
| 3 GB of model and 0.9 GB of cuBLAS on request | off by default, said before the download |
| a mixed source's transcripts wait for the owner again | accepted: the gate's rules hold for every record |
| VRAM: the worker and the embedder at once | the speech worker runs per job and ends; about 4 to 5 GB while it runs |
